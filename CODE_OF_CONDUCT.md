# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in this project a respectful,
harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or
invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality,
personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Expected behavior

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive community include:

- demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people;
- respecting differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences;
- giving and accepting constructive feedback graciously;
- taking responsibility, apologizing to those affected by mistakes, and learning
  from the experience;
- focusing on what is best for the project and its community;
- discussing technical disagreements with evidence and without personal attacks;
  and
- protecting private data, unpublished vulnerability details, and other sensitive
  information shared in confidence.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- sexualized language or imagery, sexual attention, or advances of any kind;
- trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks;
- public or private harassment;
- publishing another person's private information without explicit permission;
- deliberate intimidation, stalking, or sustained disruption;
- misrepresenting experimental evidence or another contributor's work; and
- other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces, including issues, pull
requests, reviews, discussions, commits, and project-related communication. It
also applies when an individual officially represents the project in public.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior privately to
**dochikhoa2006@gmail.com** with the subject `[Gridworld-10 conduct]`.

Include relevant links, dates, context, and any supporting material you are
comfortable sharing. Do not post sensitive conduct reports in a public issue.
Reports will be reviewed as promptly and fairly as practical. The maintainer will
respect the privacy and safety of reporters and affected community members to the
greatest extent possible, while gathering enough information to assess the report.

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing acceptable
behavior. They may edit, remove, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and
other contributions that do not align with this Code of Conduct, and will explain
moderation decisions when appropriate.

## Enforcement guidelines

Maintainers will consider the context, seriousness, impact, and history of an
incident when determining a response.

### 1. Correction

**Community impact:** Use of inappropriate language or other behavior considered
unprofessional or unwelcome.

**Consequence:** A private, written warning that explains the violation and the
expected change in behavior. A public clarification or apology may be requested
when appropriate.

### 2. Warning

**Community impact:** A violation through a single incident or a series of
actions.

**Consequence:** A warning with conditions on continued participation. The person
may be asked not to interact with involved people, including through unsolicited
contact outside project spaces, for a defined period. Violating these conditions
may result in a temporary or permanent ban.

### 3. Temporary ban

**Community impact:** A serious violation of community standards, including
sustained inappropriate behavior.

**Consequence:** A temporary ban from project interaction or public
representation. Attempts to evade the ban may lead to a permanent ban.

### 4. Permanent ban

**Community impact:** A pattern of violations, harassment, aggression toward
individuals, or disparagement of protected groups.

**Consequence:** Permanent exclusion from project interaction and public
representation.
